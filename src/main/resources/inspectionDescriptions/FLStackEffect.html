Reports stack effect mistakes that can be proved from the headers of the words a body calls (docs/LANGUAGE.md sections 7, 8 and 10.5), each an error the engine would raise when that path runs:
  • a body that ends, or reaches an exit, with a stack depth other than its header declares (E_EFFECT);
  • a word or :> that needs more values than the stack holds there (E_STACK_UNDERFLOW);
  • the two ways through if, when, unless or try leaving different depths, in a word whose output is checked;
  • a literal quotation run by each, map, filter, reduce, while, loop, times, a collector or a finder that breaks the depth rule of section 8.
The check is partial: where a body uses something it cannot follow (a word that does not resolve to one declaration, a quotation that is not literal, cond or case), it says nothing about the rest of that body. fail, fail-coerce, fail-range and words that always fail end a path.
